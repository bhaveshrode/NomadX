# NomadX Financial Regulatory Applicability Assessment

**Effective Date:** September 2, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Regulatory Categorization
NomadX functions as an **Autonomous Commerce Orchestration System** providing software automation for invoicing, dunning, and settlement tracking.

- **Non-Custodial Architecture:** NomadX does not act as a money transmitter, custodian, or bank. Funds flow directly between merchant wallets/bank accounts and customer payment providers.
- **FinCEN / MSB Scope:** NomadX software does not take custody or control of customer fiat or digital asset balances, keeping the core software service outside of Money Services Business (MSB) licensing requirements.
- **Sanctions & Compliance:** Organization onboarding includes automated checks against OFAC and global SDN lists prior to settlement execution.

## 2. Tax & Accounting Compliance
NomadX produces immutable financial audit logs and receivable reports conforming to GAAP double-entry ledger integrity checks.
